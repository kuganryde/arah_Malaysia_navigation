Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026. This is a plain-language description of how the app works, written by its builders. It is not legal advice.

In short. ARAH is free and non-profit. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your preferences, saved route, reports and cached maps stay in your browser. Some features send your search text, map area or coordinates to the public services listed below, and those services see your IP address like any website you visit.

What stays on your device

  • Preferences: avoid tolls, avoid highways, vehicle mode, language, map view.
  • Your saved route.
  • Road reports you file (they are not uploaded anywhere).
  • Cached map tiles for offline use.

This lives in your browser's local storage and cache. Clearing site data removes it.

Your location

ARAH asks your browser for your location only to show you on the map and guide you. It is used in the app and is not stored on our servers. You can refuse the permission and still search and plan routes.

AR driver assist (camera)

AR driver assist is optional and off until you turn it on. When on, the camera feed is analysed on your phone by an AI model that runs in your browser. No video or image is uploaded, recorded or saved, and the camera stops when you close AR or the trip ends. Your device speed from GPS is used the same way, on the device only. The only things stored are your choices (AR on or off, window position, alert sound).

Distance, speed and lane readings are estimates from a single camera and can be wrong. Mount the phone in a holder and never hold it while driving.

Makan & Mall (brand logos)

When you open the Makan & Mall sheet, your browser may load a brand's logo from that brand's own website (only for logos you scroll to). The brand site can see your IP address and which logo file was requested, but not your location, and no referrer is sent. Outlet searches go to Overpass (OpenStreetMap) with the map area you are viewing. You can switch logos off in the sheet, and the list then makes no requests to brand sites.

Offline copy (installed app)

When you use ARAH in a browser or install it to your home screen, a service worker saves the app's own files (and, after you first use AR, the AI model file) on your device so it opens without signal. These are public files, not your data. Your trips and locations are not added to this copy. Clear it any time in your browser's site settings, or open the app once with ?nosw=1.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Safety

ARAH is a beta. Route times, tolls and reports are estimates. Always follow road signs and local traffic laws, and do not operate the app while driving.
