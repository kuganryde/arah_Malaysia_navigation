Full description (4000 max), English
> ARAH is a free, non-profit 3D navigation app made for Malaysian roads.
> • 3D low-poly map with landmarks, terrain and buildings
> • Turn-by-turn voice in Bahasa Malaysia, English, Chinese and Tamil
> • Toll estimates in RM, highway avoidance, car / motorbike / lorry / bus / walking routes
> • Makan & Mall: find fast food, cafés and malls near you and on your route
> • AR driver assist (optional, on-device): spots vehicles and pedestrians through the camera
> • Festive themes for Malaysian celebrations
> • Works offline for cached maps; keeps the screen on while you drive
> • No accounts, no ads, nothing sold. Anonymous crash reports are off unless you switch them on.
> Beta release. Always follow road signs and local traffic laws.
